xmusic — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 31, 2026  ·  Stage-ready chord charts for Android

1. Summary — Zero Data Collection

xmusic does not collect, store, transmit, rent, or sell any personal data, usage analytics, diagnostics, or user content. The application operates completely offline on your device, requires no user account, and contains no advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs.

2. Local Storage & Android Auto Backup

  • On-Device Database: All songs, chord charts, lyrics, annotations, setlists, and display preferences you create or import are stored exclusively in a local database on your device.
  • Android Auto Backup: If you have Google Drive Backup enabled in your Android device settings, Android's system backup service may include your local xmusic song database and preferences in your private Google account backup so your library can be restored when switching devices. The developer of xmusic has no access to your backups.

3. File Import & Peer-to-Peer Sharing

  • Importing Files: When you choose to import a file (such as .xmusic, ChordPro .cho/.chopro, MusicXML .musicxml/.mxl, or iReal Pro® .html/links) via the Android system file picker or Share target, the file is parsed locally on your device. Nothing leaves your device.
  • Sharing Charts: When you export or share a song or setlist (as a PDF or .xmusic file), the file is handed directly to the Android system Share Sheet so you can send it through the app of your choice.

4. Permissions

xmusic does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location, photos, or network. File access is handled strictly on a per-file basis through Android's Storage Access Framework when initiated by you.

5. Children's Privacy

Because xmusic collects no personal information from any user, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR provisions regarding minors.
